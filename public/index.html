Award-winning hospitality craftsmanship

Guatemala Verde at the Omni Charlottesville Hotel.

Artistic Stone Design collaborated with Karn Custom Woodwork on the award-winning renovation of the Omni Charlottesville Hotel. The centerpiece is the Conservatory Bar, set within the hotel's dramatic seven-story atrium.

ASD fabricated the distinctive Guatemala Verde stonework for a design combining stone, wood, metal, glass, and mirror. Executing the complex bar required close coordination among the fabricators, designers, architects, engineers, and general contractor.
