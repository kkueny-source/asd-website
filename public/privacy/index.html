Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

Artistic Stone Design of Richmond ("Artistic Stone Design," "ASD," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when requesting a quote, selecting materials, scheduling template or installation visits, or making a payment.

Information We Collect. We collect contact and project information that you voluntarily provide to us in our showroom, by phone, by email, or through our project lead forms, including your name, service address, billing address, email address, telephone number, and project specifications.

How We Use Your Information. We use your personal information to prepare estimates and quotes, fabricate and install custom stone surfaces, schedule and confirm template, installation, pickup, or repair appointments, send invoices and payment confirmations, and provide customer support.

SMS Messaging & Mobile Privacy. If you provide your mobile phone number to Artistic Stone Design and consent to receive text messages, we may send transactional SMS notifications regarding your scheduled appointments (such as template, installation, pickup, or service reminders) and billing/invoice updates. We do not sell, rent, loan, trade, or lease any phone numbers or customer information collected through our SMS program to any third party for marketing purposes.

Non-Sharing Clause. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won’t be shared with any third parties.

Data Security & Retention. We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access or disclosure. Card payments are processed directly through Square's secure checkout and card numbers are not stored on our website.

Your Choices & Opt-Out Rights. You may opt out of receiving SMS messages from Artistic Stone Design at any time by replying STOP to any text message we send, or by contacting us at 804-594-7069 or info@asdrva.rocks. For assistance with SMS messages, reply HELP or call 804-594-7069.

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Call 804-594-7069, email info@asdrva.rocks, or write to Artistic Stone Design of Richmond, 11321 International Drive, North Chesterfield, VA 23236.
