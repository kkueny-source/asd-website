Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how Artistic Stone Design of Richmond (doing business as Artistic Stone Design, “ASD,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit artisticstonerichmond.com, request a quote, visit our showroom, or communicate with us by phone, email, or SMS text message.

Privacy & Mobile Messaging Policy

Information we collect. When you request an estimate, visit our showroom at 11321 International Drive, Richmond, VA 23236, sign a quote, schedule a field measurement (template) or countertop installation, or make a payment, we collect contact and project information you provide directly to us, such as your name, project address, email address, phone number, material selections, and billing details.

How we use your information. We use the information we collect to prepare and deliver quotes, fabricate and install custom stone surfaces, coordinate template and installation appointments, send appointment reminders and billing or payment notifications, respond to customer service inquiries, and maintain project records.

SMS text messaging & opt-in consent. If you provide your mobile phone number to Artistic Stone Design of Richmond (DBA Artistic Stone Design) and consent to receive text messages (as described in our SMS Opt-In Workflow), we may send transactional SMS notifications regarding your quote, template measurement window, installation schedule, and project invoices or payments from toll-free number (855) 310-5499. Message frequency varies based on your project schedule. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any text message we send, or get assistance by replying HELP or calling 804-594-7069.

No third-party sharing of mobile/SMS data. We do not sell, rent, loan, trade, or lease your personal information or mobile phone number to third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won’t be shared with any third parties.

Service providers & payments. We use trusted operational providers (such as Square for payment processing, Ragic for project scheduling, and Twilio for transactional notifications) strictly to perform services on our behalf under confidentiality obligations. Online payments made through our Make a Payment page are processed directly by Square under Square’s security and privacy standards.

Data security & retention. We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information and retain project records only as long as needed to fulfill your contract, honor warranty or service requests, and comply with tax and legal obligations.

Contact us. If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to update or remove your contact preferences, please contact Artistic Stone Design of Richmond (DBA Artistic Stone Design) at 11321 International Drive, Richmond, VA 23236, email info@asdrva.rocks, or call 804-594-7069.
